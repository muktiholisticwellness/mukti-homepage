Move gently. Breathe deeply. Come home to yourself.

I guide students of all levels through a practice rooted in stillness and intention. After a decade of study across Bali, Mysore, and the mountains of Colorado, I returned home with one simple belief: yoga isn't something you do — it's something you remember. My classes blend Hatha fundamentals with slow flow sequences, creating space for both the beginner and the seasoned practitioner to arrive exactly where they are.
